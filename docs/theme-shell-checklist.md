# Theme Shell acceptance checklist

Check a new or changed **Theme Shell** against this list before it ships. The contract it is written against is `ThemeShellProps` in `src/features/theme/theme-shell.ts`; a **Theme** is registered in `src/features/theme/themes.ts`, with its id in `theme-ids.ts`. The shared tests in `src/features/theme/shells/theme-shells.test.ts` run against the shell of every registered Theme and of the test fixture Theme (`tests/theme-fixture`), and `themes.test.ts` checks each Theme's stylesheet and that the registered Themes use every recipe value; together they cover the automatic part of the list.

## Contract

- The shell arranges what the host gives it: every navigation entry, every sidebar card once, the page content, the footer and the floating actions. It adds no card and drops none. Which cards a page shows is decided by the host (`sidebarCardsFor`), because the cache tags of a page assume them. The one exception is also decided by the host: a shell that lists a page kind in `identityInFirstScreen` gets no identity card there.
- Where the cards go is the shell's choice: on either side of the content, below it, above the footer, or, on wide screens only, before it. It may stand them in more than one group; a group keeps the order the cards were given in, and a group placed before the content comes before it in the document too, so the document reads in the order a wide screen shows. Below 1024 px every shell is a single column with all the cards after the content; no shell hides them behind a drawer or a menu. A card that renders nothing leaves no gap, and neither does a group of such cards.
- A card is never told where it stands. Each has a narrow form, for a column beside the content, and a wide, low one on the same markup, and takes one or the other from the width of the CSS container named `card` around it: the profile card lies on its side from 20rem; the categories and tags become pills from 20rem, two rows under their title, and from 34rem one row beside it, with 「更多」 only while entries are cut off; the latest Moments stand two abreast from 28rem and three from 42rem, and from 56rem their title leads the row. A shell that gives cards more room than a narrow column makes the slot of each card that container in its stylesheet (`container: card / inline-size`, one slot per card, as `.immersive-card` in `shells/immersive/immersive.css`) and gives the slot the width the card is to have. A shell that declares no such container gets the narrow form at every width, as 「经典」 and 「名片」 do.
- A Theme differs in layout only through its shell and its **Theme Recipe**. A form the recipe offers includes its arrangement (a grid of tiles is a form of the **Post** list, not a second setting beside the card form). Every value of every recipe item is used by at least one registered Theme: add a form together with the first Theme that uses it, and remove it when none does.
- The shell never looks at the path. It reads `pageKind`: `home`, `inner`, `post` for a **Post**'s page, or `focused`. A focused page gets no cards and shows only its content. A Post's page gets the cards of an inner page; a shell may lay it out apart from the other inner pages, and one that does not treats it as `inner`.
- The shell wraps its tree in `FirstScreenProvider` with the height of its first screen, which back to top, the table of contents and any navbar hiding read.
- The shell's modules import no CSS. Its styles are the Theme's `stylesheet`, which the `_public` route links only while that Theme is selected; styles more than one shell needs live with the shared component instead (the menu rows in `mobile-menu.css`).
- The stylesheet sets its design variables (`--theme-*`, never `--fuwari-*`, never `!important`) and the shell positions on `:root[data-theme="<id>"]` only. Public pages carry that attribute on `<html>` and `/admin` never does, so dialogs, toasts and the table of contents, which render outside the shell, read the values, and the **Admin** does not.
- The shell positions are `--shell-floating-actions-left` and the five that place the table of contents of a Post, each from the shell's own media queries; the shared components know no breakpoint of a Theme. `--shell-toc-side` is `block` where the list stands beside the article and `none` where it does not; `--shell-toc-compact` is `flex` where the button in the floating column opens it instead and `none` where it does not. The side list is fixed at `--shell-toc-top` from the top of the window, starts `--shell-toc-left` from the left edge of the article column (`100%` is the column's width, a negative value is the left gutter) and is `--shell-toc-width` wide, with no padding of its own; where the shell's footer region runs under it, it ends short of the footer by itself. Back to top and the page's actions are fixed to the window unless the shell sets `--shell-floating-actions-position: static` and stands them in a box it places itself, as 「沉浸」 does to rest them above its footer at the end of a page. `theme-shell.ts` describes each.
- The shell marks its root `data-theme-shell="<id>"` and its regions `data-shell-region="body" | "sidebar" | "main" | "footer"`, with every group of cards a `sidebar` and, like the footer, inside the body. The render-error fallback in `status-page.css` uses only these.

## By hand

- **Personalization.** Every shared personalization field in **Site Config** shows: the banner image and its focus, the avatar, the hue, the brand mark and the tagline. Change each one and see it change.
- **Navigation.** Every navigation entry, the custom links among them, external links open in a new tab, search, the Colour Scheme toggle, the language switch, the account entry (signed out, signed in, signed in as the **Admin**) and the compact menu all work, with the mouse and with the keyboard.
- **Widths.** Each public page at 1440, 820 and 390 px wide, at the top and scrolled: nothing overflows, overlaps or is cut off, and the table of contents and back to top sit in place, at the end of the page too: neither lies on the footer, and back to top can be clicked there. On a Post with a long table of contents, also one pixel below and at the width where the side list takes over from the button: the shell's choice of the two shows, and the side list clears the article and the edge of the window.
- **Cards.** In a shell that declares the `card` container: each kind of page at 1440, 1024, 1023, 820 and 390 px, every card in the form the width of its slot calls for. Resize across those widths with 「更多」 unused and used. With five categories and with forty tags, 「更多」 is there exactly while entries are cut off, on the first paint too (reload with JavaScript disabled on a page that does not stream the cards, such as `/posts`), and the keyboard reaches only what can be seen. On a site with no Moments, no categories or no tags there is no gap where the card would be.
- **Colour Schemes.** Each of the above in light and in dark.
- **Reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, nothing slides or fades on load, on navigation or on scroll beyond what the shared components already allow.
- **No jump on load.** The first screen has its final size from the first paint: no layout shift while the banner image, fonts or the session load, and none on a reload in the middle of a page.
- **Focused pages.** Sign-in, profile, the friend-link form, not found and a route error show their content without sidebar cards; so does a page whose content fails to render.
- **Only its stylesheet, on a production build.** `bun scripts/check-theme-styles.ts` builds with the test fixture Theme (`THEME_FIXTURE=1`), serves the build on port 3100 against the local database (it needs a `.dev.vars` whose `DOMAIN` is a domain name, not localhost), selects each Theme through the Admin API, and checks that `/` links the selected Theme's stylesheet and no other's and that `/admin` links none. `--no-build` reuses the last build. It is not part of `bun run check`. While it is built, also look with JavaScript disabled (the first screen is styled) and on a throttled network (no unstyled frame).
