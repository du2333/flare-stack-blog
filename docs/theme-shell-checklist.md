# Theme Shell acceptance checklist

Check a new or changed **Theme Shell** against this list before it ships. The contract it is written against is `ThemeShellProps` in `src/features/theme/theme-shell.ts`; a **Theme** is registered in `src/features/theme/themes.ts`, with its id in `theme-ids.ts`. The shared tests in `src/features/theme/shells/theme-shells.test.ts` run against the shell of every registered Theme and of the test fixture Theme (`tests/theme-fixture`), and `themes.test.ts` checks each Theme's stylesheet and that the registered Themes use every recipe value; together they cover the automatic part of the list.

## Contract

- The shell arranges what the host gives it: every navigation entry, every sidebar card once, the page content, the footer and the floating actions. It adds no card and drops none. A shell that itself shows the site identity at some widths (avatar, author, description, in a rail for instance) may hide the identity card at exactly those widths; the card stays in the page and shows wherever the shell's own identity does not. Which cards a page shows is decided by the host (`sidebarCardsFor`), because the cache tags of a page assume them. The one exception is also decided by the host: a shell that lists a page kind in `identityInFirstScreen` gets no identity card there.
- A navigation entry (`NavOption` in `src/components/layout/layout-props.ts`) is a link or a group: a name over one level of links (at most six), which leads nowhere itself. A shell shows a group in one of three ways, and never makes it a link. In a bar: the shared `NavGroupMenu` (`src/components/layout/nav-group-menu.tsx`: a trigger and a menu of the links on the float surface), as 「经典」, 「名片」 and 「沉浸」 do. The menu's rows (`.nav-group-item`: the link's name, where a link out of the site leads, a mark at the end) are the Theme's to draw in its stylesheet, as the trigger is: 「名片」 numbers them as an index and says where a link leads, 「沉浸」 makes them pills of the bar's make. A Theme that draws nothing has 「经典」's rows (`nav-group-menu.css`). In a vertical list (the rail of 「侧栏」): a disclosure that opens in place, a row with the group's name and a chevron over its links as rows set in under it. Or the group's links in its place (`flattenNavOptions` in `nav-options.ts`). A disclosure keeps four things: its row is a control from `src/components/ui` (the `Button` with `DisclosureChevron` and `DisclosurePanel`, not a bare `button`) carrying `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that names the panel; while it is closed its links are not reachable (the panel is `inert` and hidden from assistive technology, as `DisclosurePanel` does it); a group is open when it holds the current link, and opens when the current link comes to lie in it from another page, while a group the reader has closed stays closed for as long as the current link stays in it; and the state is kept by something that survives a reorder of the navigation (a group's id is its position in Site Config, so not that), and drops with the group. Where a list marks the current row with one pill that glides, the pill stands where the row is among the rows on show, so it is right on the first paint and moves with the rows while a group opens or closes. The compact menu (`MobileMenu`) shows a group as its name, which leads nowhere, over its links, always open, and a footer that lists the navigation lists the links in the group's place. `currentNavLink` names the one link the page belongs to, the longest address winning, and `isNavOptionCurrent` marks that link and the group that holds it.
- Where the cards go is the shell's choice: on either side of the content, below it, above the footer, or, on wide screens only, before it. It may stand them in more than one group; a group keeps the order the cards were given in, and a group placed before the content comes before it in the document too, so the document reads in the order a wide screen shows. Below 1024 px every shell is a single column with all the cards after the content; no shell hides them behind a drawer or a menu. A card that renders nothing leaves no gap, and neither does a group of such cards.
- A card is never told where it stands. Each has a narrow form, for a column beside the content, and a wide, low one on the same markup, and takes one or the other from the width of the CSS container named `card` around it: the profile card lies on its side from 20rem; the categories and tags become pills from 20rem, two rows under their title, and from 34rem one row beside it, with 「更多」 only while entries are cut off; the latest Moments stand two abreast from 28rem and three from 42rem, and from 56rem their title leads the row. A shell that gives cards more room than a narrow column makes the slot of each card that container in its stylesheet (`container: card / inline-size`, one slot per card, as `.immersive-card` in `shells/immersive/immersive.css`) and gives the slot the width the card is to have. A shell that declares no such container gets the narrow form at every width, as 「经典」 does.
- A Theme differs in layout only through its shell and its **Theme Recipe**. A form the recipe offers includes its arrangement (a grid of tiles is a form of the **Post** list, not a second setting beside the card form). Every value of every recipe item is used by at least one registered Theme: add a form together with the first Theme that uses it, and remove it when none does.
- The shell never looks at the path. It reads `pageKind`: `home`, `inner`, `post` for a **Post**'s page, or `focused`. A focused page gets no cards and shows only its content. A Post's page gets the cards of an inner page; a shell may lay it out apart from the other inner pages, and one that does not treats it as `inner`. The host tells a shell two kinds: `pageKind` is the page the address names and changes with the click; `pageOnScreen` is the page `main` holds, which is replaced only once the next page has loaded, in one commit with it. What answers the click may follow `pageKind` (a first screen that folds away); everything laid out around the page follows `pageOnScreen` (a reading column, where the cards stand, a slot for the table of contents), so no page is seen in the layout of another, however long the next one takes to load.
- The shell wraps its tree in `FirstScreenProvider` with the height of its first screen, which the table of contents and any navbar hiding read. A page that has no first screen says 0: the table of contents of a Post is then there from the top of the page.
- The shell's modules import no CSS. Its styles are the Theme's `stylesheet`, which the `_public` route links only while that Theme is selected; styles more than one shell needs live with the shared component instead (the rows of the compact menu in `mobile-menu.css`, the rows of a group's menu in `nav-group-menu.css`); they are 「经典」's look, and a Theme's stylesheet draws its own over them inside its own panel or menu.
- The compact menu is the shared rows (`MobileMenu`: every navigation entry, then the account, then the Colour Scheme and the language) in a panel whose form is the shell's: a card under the bar (「经典」), a page of its own under the bar with the entries set as titles (「名片」), a sheet of frost (「沉浸」), the rail brought in as a drawer (「侧栏」, the `Sheet` from `src/components/ui`, with the entries led by the rail's icons through `lead`). `useMenuPanel` marks the panel `data-compact="true"` while it is the compact menu and not the account menu that shares it, and with `coversPage` keeps the page behind a panel as large as the window from scrolling. Whatever the form, the menu closes on a navigation, on Escape and on a press beside it, takes focus when it opens and gives it back to its button, and scrolls inside itself when the navigation is longer than the window.
- The stylesheet sets its design variables (`--theme-*`, never `--fuwari-*`, never `!important`) and the shell positions on `:root[data-theme="<id>"]` only. Public pages carry that attribute on `<html>` and `/admin` never does, so dialogs, toasts and the table of contents, which render outside the shell, read the values, and the **Admin** does not.
- A Theme has a surface language of its own, said in the design variables alone: the ground of the page (`--theme-ground`, which the shell paints and the document root has behind it), what content sits on (`--theme-surface`, with its corners and its shadow or edge), what is tonal and what is solid, and what floats (`--theme-float-bg`, `-shadow`, `-backdrop`, `-radius`, with `--theme-dialog-radius` and `-shadow` and `--theme-toast-pad`). Menus, popovers, toasts, dialogs, sheets, back to top and the bar of a Theme preview read the float values, so none of them is styled per Theme anywhere else; the one exception is the Theme's own navigation, the rows of a group's menu and the compact menu (above). A tag or a category as a pill reads `--theme-chip-bg`, `--theme-chip-edge` and `--theme-chip-count-bg` where the Theme sets them, and is the tonal fill where it does not. A Theme that leaves a group at its default looks like 「经典」 there; check each inner page, not only the home page, for that.
- The shell positions are `--shell-floating-actions-left` and the five that place the table of contents of a Post, each from the shell's own media queries; the shared components know no breakpoint of a Theme. `--shell-toc-side` is `block` where the side list shows and `none` where it does not; `--shell-toc-compact` is `flex` where the button in the floating column opens it instead and `none` where it does not. The side list has its place from one of two sources. Where the shell gives it no slot (below), it is fixed at `--shell-toc-top` from the top of the window, starts `--shell-toc-left` from the left edge of the article column (`100%` is the column's width, a negative value is the left gutter) and is `--shell-toc-width` wide, with no padding of its own; it follows the column when the window is resized and when the column changes width without that (a rail that comes in, a reading column the shell switches to), and where the shell's footer region runs under it, it ends short of the footer by itself. Back to top and the page's actions are fixed to the window unless the shell sets `--shell-floating-actions-position: static` and stands them in a box it places itself, as 「沉浸」 does to rest them above its footer at the end of a page. From lg each of them is 3.75rem a side unless the shell sets `--shell-floating-actions-size`, as 「侧栏」 does where it rests them in the foot of its rail. A shell that keeps a part of the window's width for a frame of its own, such as a rail, says how far from either edge of the window the content starts (`--shell-content-inset-left`, `--shell-content-inset-right`): the bar of a Theme preview is centred in the rest and never lies on the frame. `theme-shell.ts` describes each.
- The other source is a slot the shell gives the side list in its own tree: one `TocSlot` (`src/features/theme/components/toc-slot.tsx`), with nothing in it, in a box such as a rail. While it is mounted the list renders into it, fills its box and scrolls within it (the slot needs a definite height for that), shows from the top of the page, and reads neither `--shell-toc-top`, `--shell-toc-left` nor `--shell-toc-width`. `--shell-toc-side` and `--shell-toc-compact` still apply: at every width where the stylesheet hides the slot it sets them to `none` and `flex`, so the button opens the list there. A Post without headings leaves the slot empty; the page carries `[data-table-of-contents]` from the first paint exactly while it has a table of contents, so the stylesheet shows something else in the slot's place with `:has()`. The slot stays mounted for as long as a Post's page is in `main`: render it on every page, or switch it with `pageOnScreen`, never with `pageKind`. The shared shell tests check that a shell has at most one slot, inside its body, that the list stands in it, and that a Post without headings leaves it empty.
- The shell marks its root `data-theme-shell="<id>"` and its regions `data-shell-region="body" | "sidebar" | "main" | "footer"`, with every group of cards a `sidebar` and, like the footer, inside the body. The render-error fallback in `status-page.css` uses only these.

## By hand

- **Personalization.** Every shared personalization field in **Site Config** shows: the banner image and its focus, the avatar, the hue, the brand mark and the tagline. Change each one and see it change.
- **Navigation.** Every navigation entry, the custom links among them and the links of a group (with at least one group of two links), external links open in a new tab, search, the Colour Scheme toggle, the language switch, the account entry (signed out, signed in, signed in as the **Admin**) and the compact menu all work, with the mouse and with the keyboard. A group in a bar opens on a click and while the mouse rests on it, and closes a moment after the mouse leaves; Tab reaches it, Enter or Space opens it, the arrows move between its links, and Escape closes it back onto it. A group in a vertical list opens and closes on a click, Enter or Space, in place; closed, Tab passes over its links; opened by the page it holds, from the address, the home page and a link in a Post alike. A group shows as current while one of its links is the page (the pill on the group's row while it is closed, on the link's own row while it is open), and its menu or its rows read in both Colour Schemes.
- **Widths.** Each public page at 1440, 820 and 390 px wide, at the top and scrolled: nothing overflows, overlaps or is cut off, and the table of contents and back to top sit in place, at the end of the page too: neither lies on the footer, and back to top can be clicked there. On a Post with a long table of contents, also one pixel below and at the width where the side list takes over from the button: the shell's choice of the two shows, and the side list clears the article and the edge of the window. Where the shell changes the width of the article column without a resize of the window (a reading column on the way into a Post, a rail that comes in), go from another page into a Post and scroll: the side list stands beside the column as it is now. In a shell that gives the list a slot, also at the widths where the slot is hidden (the button shows and opens the list), on a Post whose list is longer than the slot (it scrolls within the slot, and the current section stays in view there), on a Post without headings (what the shell shows in the slot's place is there from the first paint, and stays once the page has loaded), and on the way into a Post and out of it (the list never shows at the fixed place, not for a frame).
- **Cards.** In a shell that declares the `card` container: each kind of page at 1440, 1024, 1023, 820 and 390 px, every card in the form the width of its slot calls for. Resize across those widths with 「更多」 unused and used. With five categories and with forty tags, 「更多」 is there exactly while entries are cut off, on the first paint too (reload with JavaScript disabled on a page that does not stream the cards, such as `/posts`), and the keyboard reaches only what can be seen. On a site with no Moments, no categories or no tags there is no gap where the card would be.
- **Floats.** Open a menu (the language switch), a dialog (deleting a Moment as the **Admin**) and a toast (sharing a Moment), with the bar of a Theme preview showing: each has the Theme's fill, corners and shadow, and text on a float that lets the page through reads over a bright image and over a dark one.
- **Colour Schemes.** Each of the above in light and in dark.
- **Reduced motion.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, nothing slides or fades on load, on navigation or on scroll beyond what the shared components already allow.
- **No jump on load.** The first screen has its final size from the first paint: no layout shift while the banner image, fonts or the session load, and none on a reload in the middle of a page.
- **Focused pages.** Sign-in, profile, the friend-link form, not found and a route error show their content without sidebar cards; so does a page whose content fails to render.
- **Only its stylesheet, on a production build.** `bun scripts/check-theme-styles.ts` builds with the test fixture Theme (`THEME_FIXTURE=1`), serves the build on port 3100 against the local database (it needs a `.dev.vars` whose `DOMAIN` is a domain name, not localhost), selects each Theme through the Admin API, and checks that `/` links the selected Theme's stylesheet and no other's and that `/admin` links none. `--no-build` reuses the last build. It is not part of `bun run check`. While it is built, also look with JavaScript disabled (the first screen is styled) and on a throttled network (no unstyled frame).
